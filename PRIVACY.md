# Codex Binder Lane Privacy Policy

Effective: September 1, 2026

Codex Binder Lane is an open-source, skills-only plugin published by Jacob Vogan. It is a local control and evidence layer for protein-binder campaigns. The publisher does not operate a Binder Lane server, user-account system, analytics service, or advertising network.

## Data the plugin processes

At the user's direction, Binder Lane may process:

- target protein sequences, structures, chain and residue identifiers, target-site definitions, and related scientific files;
- campaign plans, budgets, provider choices, authorization records, qualification records, and user instructions;
- candidate sequences, structures, metrics, images, reports, receipts, hashes, logs, and other campaign artifacts; and
- filesystem paths and coarse environment facts needed to locate selected tools and files.

The publisher does not intentionally collect personal data through the plugin. Do not provide payment-card data, government identifiers, passwords, API keys, authentication secrets, protected health information, or clinical records to Binder Lane.

## How data is used

Binder Lane uses the supplied information to plan and validate campaigns, lock target and site identity, select separately available tools, record authorization and cost boundaries, verify receipts and artifacts, and prepare the requested scientific delivery.

The plugin writes campaign files to locations in the user's environment. It does not send telemetry or campaign contents to the publisher.

## External services and recipients

Binder Lane can coordinate separately installed companion tools, hosted APIs, or compute providers. Data is sent to an external service only when the user selects or authorizes that route under the controls of the current ChatGPT or Codex environment. Before transfer, the workflow identifies the provider, data classification, expected cost, and material authorization boundary.

External services process data under their own privacy policies and terms. OpenAI and the environment in which the plugin runs may also process conversation content and files under the policies and settings that apply to the user's OpenAI account. The publisher does not receive those contents merely because Binder Lane is installed.

## Retention

The publisher retains no plugin telemetry or campaign data because the plugin sends none to the publisher. Local plans, artifacts, receipts, and reports remain in the user's selected workspace until the user deletes them. A separately selected service may retain submitted data according to its own policy, account configuration, and the route approved by the user.

## User controls

Users can:

- keep planning and validation local and decline external execution;
- classify inputs as public, private, or restricted and withhold confidential material;
- approve or reject each material provider, spend, license, and data-transfer decision;
- inspect, copy, edit, or delete local campaign files; and
- disable or uninstall the plugin and revoke access granted to companion services through those services' controls.

## Security

Binder Lane uses scoped file contracts, hashes, path checks, secret-pattern rejection, authorization gates, and fail-closed validation to reduce accidental disclosure and artifact confusion. No software or transfer method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Users remain responsible for choosing appropriate environments and providers for confidential or unpublished research data.

## Children

Binder Lane is a scientific research tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this repository with a revised effective date. Privacy questions and requests can be opened at <https://github.com/jvogan/codex-binder-lane/issues>.
